2. Choose the markup

Minimum 40%. Markup is added to cost; it is different from gross margin. Enter a value from 40 to 1,000, with up to two decimals.

Planning price = total cost × (1 + markup ÷ 100). The price is rounded up to the next cent so rounding cannot reduce the requested markup.

Move from a scenario to a reviewable quote

  1. Confirm the scope

    Record the task, asset details, location, access requirements and service window. Use the datacenter service-request guide to collect the scope.

  2. Validate the supplier evidence

    Check the supplier’s written cost, currency, validity, coverage and exclusions. Make taxes, travel, freight and import costs explicit; do not treat unknown costs as zero.

  3. Review the commercial price

    Reconcile the full cost and apply at least 40% markup. Review scope, taxes and approvals before presenting a client quotation.

Worked example

If the complete recorded cost is 1,000 and markup is 40%, the planning price is 1,400. The difference is 400, which is 28.57% of the price. This arithmetic example is not a market rate or a promised profit.
